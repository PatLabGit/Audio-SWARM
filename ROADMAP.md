# Audio-SWARM — Development Roadmap

Here’s a look at what I’m building next for Audio-SWARM! Plans shift as things evolve, so treat this as a living list rather than set-in-stone promises.

## On Deck

Features planned for upcoming releases.

- [ ] **Standalone Binaries:** Provide downloadable packages for each operating system:
    - Linux: `.AppImage` and/or `.deb`
    - Windows: `.exe`
    - macOS: `.dmg`
    
- [ ] **Sinusoidal Waveform:** Introduce sinusoidal waveform styles in both Sound Wave Maker and Circular Waveform.
- [ ] **Vector Export:** Add SVG and PDF export support for scalable, high-quality waveform artwork and professional printing.

## In the pipeline

- [ ] **Pre-Made Disc Labels:** Add ready-to-use label templates for MiniDisc, CD, Mini CD, DVD, Blu-ray, vinyl records in various sizes and speeds, Flexi Discs, Shellac Records and Acetate Discs.

*Non-exhaustive list:*

- MiniDisc
- CD (120 mm)
- Mini CD (80 mm)
- DVD (120 mm)
- Mini DVD (80 mm)
- Blu-ray Disc (120 mm)
- Mini Blu-ray Disc (80 mm)
- Vinyl Record 12" (305 mm): 33⅓, 45 and 78 RPM
- Vinyl Record 10" (254 mm): 33⅓, 45 and 78 RPM
- Vinyl Record 7" (178 mm): 33⅓ and 45 RPM
- Vinyl Record 5" (127 mm)
- Flexi Disc
- Shellac Record
- Acetate / Lacquer Disc

## Polishing & Tweaks

Existing features and components that need further development.

- [ ] **GUI Redesign:** The current user interface needs significant improvement. Several design ideas are being considered, potentially leading to a complete GUI refactoring.
- [ ] **Vertical Waveform Gradient:** Improve gradient rendering quality and processing speed.

## Bug Squashing

Known issues and bugs scheduled for investigation or correction.

- [ ] **Bug Reports:** Found a bug? Please report it through the [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/PatLabGit/Audio-SWARM/issues) page.

## Ideas & Brainstorming

Ideas and features being evaluated. These are not confirmed commitments.

### Visualization & Artwork Customization
- [ ] **Interactive Audio Preview:** Allow users to move the playback position (playhead) to explore different sections of a track and preview how the waveform visualization changes.
- [ ] **Background Image Support:** Allow users to import an image as a background for their waveform artwork, with options for positioning, scaling and fitting.
- [ ] **Additional Waveform Styles:** Expand the selection of available waveform shapes and visualization styles.

### Audio Visualization
- [ ] **Frequency-Based Visualization:** Additional visualization modes based on frequency distribution rather than amplitude alone.
- [ ] **Spectral Centroid:** Visual representation of the audio's spectral brightness.
- [ ] **Tempo & Beat Density:** Visualizations driven by rhythm, tempo and beat activity.
- [ ] **Harmonic Visualization:** Explore ways to represent harmonic content and tonal characteristics.
- [ ] **Stereo Comparison:** Additional ways to visualize differences between left and right audio channels.

### Workflow & Customization
- [ ] **Custom Presets:** Save and reload personal configurations for waveform styles, colours, gradients and rendering settings.
- [ ] **Export Presets:** Ready-to-use settings for common resolutions, aspect ratios and printing formats.
- [ ] **Visualization Templates:** Predefined combinations of rendering styles and colour schemes.
- [ ] **Batch Processing Profiles:** Save different processing configurations for repeated batch operations.

### Additional Possibilities
- [ ] **Audio Information Panel:** Display technical information such as duration, sample rate, channels, bitrate and peak levels.
- [ ] **Enhanced Preview Controls:** More control over preview scaling, positioning and comparison.
- [ ] **Command-Line Interface:** Allow selected Audio-SWARM operations to be launched from a terminal or external scripts.

---

*This roadmap is maintained as development progresses. Priorities and plans may change.*
